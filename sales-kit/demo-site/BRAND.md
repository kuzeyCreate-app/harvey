# Dunewind Air Care: brand sheet (fictional demo brand)

Concept demo for small AC servicing companies in the UAE. The brand is invented, so a prospect can swap in their own name, number, prices and licence without any trademark concern.

## Voice
Plain, calm and exact, like a technician who tells you the price before he starts.

## Colors
| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | #0B2233 | Text, dark sections |
| Sea | #14627C | Primary links, key numbers |
| Deep sea | #0E4A5E | Hover and pressed states |
| Glacier | #E6F2F6 | Alternate section background |
| Frost | #F6FAFB | Page background |
| Sun | #F2B33D | The one call-to-action color and the "outside heat" number |

## Typefaces
- Display: Bricolage Grotesque (headlines and big numerals)
- Body: Instrument Sans (text, forms, UI)

## Signature element
The temperature pair in the hero: 46° outside, cooling to 23° inside. It is the one orchestrated motion on the page and it is skipped for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

## Rules for this site
- Sample prices, areas and warranty terms are placeholders to be replaced by the client.
- No customer reviews are shown. Real reviews go in the reserved slot once the client has some.
- Smooth scrolling (Lenis) is on by default and off for reduced-motion visitors.
