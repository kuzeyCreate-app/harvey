Questions

Short answers.

How often should an AC be serviced in the UAE?

Units here run almost all year, so many technicians suggest a service every three to four months. Ask for the plan that fits how hard your unit works.

Do you quote before starting?

Always. You get the price in writing on WhatsApp and nothing starts until you approve it.

I live in an apartment. Do you need anything from the building?

Some buildings want a visitor or work permit for technicians. Tell us your building when you book and we will say what is needed.

What if the problem comes back?

Repairs carry 90 days of workmanship cover in this demo. The client sets the real terms.
